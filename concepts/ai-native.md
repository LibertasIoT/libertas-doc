# AI-native by design

**Build for people.**\
**No AI code. No AI design. Not even a thought about AI.**\
AI comes free with it.

## Your App works with AI on day one

As long as you can use the App, AI can use it the same way, if you let it:
the same requests, the same checks, the same access. No AI code.
→ [AI tool calls](../reference/protocols.md#ai-tool-calls)

## No test scripts. AI tests your App

An AI agent drives your App through Studio: real requests, every reply in
order, nothing dropped. It has the same access as the Hub login it uses, and
there are no test scripts to write or maintain.
→ [Automated QA](../reference/app-contract.md#automated-qa-through-studio-mcp)

## AI fills in the form. Never type the same thing twice

Take a photo of a paper form, or drop in a PDF. AI fills in the fields; you
check and submit.
One declaration turns it on.
→ [AI Import](../reference/llm-document-transformation.md)

## One-word navigation. Say a word, and AI opens the right screen out of hundreds

"Tesla." The word appears nowhere in the tax App, yet AI opens the deductions
page where the clean vehicle credit lives, from the links the App already
offers.
→ [AI Search](../reference/protocols.md#ai-navigation-and-ai-search)

## What it takes

A language model connected to your Hub; for QA, an AI agent with Studio's MCP
tools. Nothing in your code.
